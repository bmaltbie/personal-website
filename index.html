Bio Research Writing Experience

Hi, I'm Ben! I'm currently a Member of Technical Staff at Geodesic Research in London, researching pre-training and mid-training safety interventions. Before that, I was a Pivotal research fellow studying personas and multi-agent interactions under Cozmin Ududec at UK AISI.

I finished my master's degree at MIT earlier this year, where I studied EECS and entrepreneurship and was part of MIT AI Alignment (MAIA). Before grad school, I spent four years as a software engineer at Amazon, working on routing and optimization for their truck and train fleets across North America and Europe. And before that, I studied computer science and economics at Georgetown, where I was a George F. Baker Scholar.

Outside of my work at Geodesic, my research interests include societal impacts of AI (unintentional human influence), behavioral evals (sycophancy and personas), and multi-agent communication.

Hobbies: I love building mechanical keyboards, playing sports (badminton, archery, skiing, squash), and cooking & baking. I attended culinary school part-time while working in New York, though I never quite finished. Happy to chat!
